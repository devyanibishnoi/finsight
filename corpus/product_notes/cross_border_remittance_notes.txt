FinBank PRODUCT TEAM — INTERNAL NOTE
Subject: Cross-Border Remittance — What We Support and What We Don't
Author: Rohan Verma, Product Lead (International)
Date: December 15, 2024
Audience: Support team

---

We get a lot of confused tickets about what FinBank supports for international money transfers. This note is the definitive guide. Please stop guessing and refer here.

WHAT FinBank CURRENTLY SUPPORTS (OUTWARD REMITTANCE FROM INDIA)
1. FinBank does not offer outward international remittance directly. We do not have an SWIFT/wire transfer feature. Customers who want to send money abroad cannot do it from FinBank.
2. FinBank's debit card can be used for international card transactions (online purchases, travel) once international transactions are enabled. This is card-based forex spending, not remittance.
3. The LRS (Liberalised Remittance Scheme) limit of USD 2,50,000 per financial year applies to all international card spends.

WHY WE DON'T SUPPORT OUTWARD REMITTANCE YET
Per RBI Master Directions on PPIs, a non-bank PPI issuer (which is our current licensing status) cannot offer outward remittance. Outward remittance requires either an AD-I (Authorised Dealer Category I) bank licence or a tie-up with an AD-I bank. We are in the process of obtaining our banking licence upgrade. Until then, refer customers to established remittance services.

WHAT ABOUT UPI FOR INTERNATIONAL PAYMENTS?
UPI is currently available for outward payments to:
- UAE (specific banks, via NPCI-AANI corridor).
- Singapore (PayNow-UPI corridor).
- Bhutan (DrukPay-UPI corridor).
These are available only for Tier 2 customers and are limited to ₹60,000 equivalent per transaction (NPCI limit for international UPI corridors).
No other countries are currently supported via UPI, despite what you may have read in the news. The RBI approvals are issued per-corridor and are not blanket.

INWARD REMITTANCE (MONEY COMING INTO INDIA)
FinBank accounts can receive inward remittances from abroad. If a customer's family is sending money from a foreign country to their FinBank account, this is supported via SWIFT/NEFT through our partner bank. The customer should share their IFSC code and account number. Note: FinBank accounts don't have their own IFSC. The inward remittance IFSC is that of our partner bank (currently: HDFC Bank, IFSC: HDFC0001234 — confirm with Operations for latest).

COMMON WRONG ANSWERS AGENTS ARE GIVING (PLEASE STOP)
"You can use FinBank to send money to the US." — WRONG. We don't support this.
"Just enable international transactions on your card." — Correct for card spends, WRONG if customer wants to remit.
"Use UPI to send money abroad." — Only valid for UAE, Singapore, Bhutan. Not a blanket answer.

WHAT TO TELL CUSTOMERS WHO WANT TO REMIT ABROAD
Acknowledge the request, explain FinBank's current limitation, and suggest they use an authorised remittance provider (Wise, Western Union, or their bank's remittance service). Log the request as a feature feedback ticket.
