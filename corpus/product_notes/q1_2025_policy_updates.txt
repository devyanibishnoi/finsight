FinBank PRODUCT TEAM — INTERNAL NOTE
Subject: Q1 2025 Policy and Limit Updates — What's Changed
Author: Neha Joshi, Policy & Compliance Lead
Date: March 20, 2025
Audience: All support staff, product team

---

This memo summarizes all policy and limit changes that took effect in Q1 2025 (January–March 2025). Please update your mental model accordingly and stop referencing the old values.

1. UPI COOLING PERIOD FOR NEW ACCOUNTS — EFFECTIVE MARCH 1, 2025
NPCI issued a circular in February 2025 requiring all payment apps to impose a cooling period on new UPI accounts. Effective March 1, 2025, all accounts registered on FinBank within the last 24 hours are limited to ₹5,000 per UPI transaction and ₹10,000 per day. This applies regardless of KYC tier.
OLD LIMIT: No cooling period for KYC-verified accounts.
NEW LIMIT: ₹5,000 / ₹10,000 for first 24 hours, regardless of KYC status.

2. IMPS FEE REMOVAL — EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2025
We removed the IMPS transaction fee for amounts up to ₹1,00,000. This was previously ₹5 per transaction.
OLD: ₹5 fee for IMPS up to ₹1,00,000.
NEW: Free for all IMPS up to ₹1,00,000. Fee of ₹15 only for amounts above ₹1,00,000.

3. ANNUAL DEBIT CARD FEE WAIVER THRESHOLD CHANGE — EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 1, 2025
The waiver condition for the ₹199 annual debit card fee was changed.
OLD: Waived if annual card spending exceeded ₹25,000.
NEW: Waived if annual card spending exceeds ₹50,000.
This affects renewal cycles. Customers who renewed before February 1, 2025 are on the old threshold for their current year.

4. EMI FORECLOSURE FEE WAIVER PERIOD EXTENDED — EFFECTIVE JANUARY 15, 2025
OLD: 2% foreclosure fee within the first 3 months. No fee after 3 months.
NEW: 2% foreclosure fee within the first 6 months. No fee after 6 months.
This change applies only to loans disbursed on or after January 15, 2025. Older loans remain on the old 3-month rule.

5. INTERNATIONAL TRANSACTION DAILY LIMIT INCREASE — EFFECTIVE MARCH 15, 2025
OLD: ₹75,000 combined international daily limit.
NEW: ₹1,00,000 combined international daily limit (all international channels combined).
This brings us in line with peer neobanks and aligns with the revised LRS framework.

6. DORMANCY FEE CAP INTRODUCED — EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 1, 2025
A customer reported that their dormant account balance was being entirely consumed by dormancy fees (a small-balance account edge case). We introduced a cap.
OLD: ₹100 per quarter, no cap.
NEW: ₹100 per quarter, capped at 25% of account balance. So a customer with ₹200 balance will be charged max ₹50 per quarter, not ₹100.

7. WHAT HAS NOT CHANGED
UPI per-transaction limits (₹1,00,000 for Tier 2), account maintenance fee (still zero), wallet load limits, re-KYC timelines, and the dispute resolution SLAs are all unchanged in Q1 2025.
