FinBank PRODUCT TEAM — INTERNAL NOTE
Subject: Prepaid Wallet Edge Cases
Author: Sneha Kulkarni, Product Lead (Wallet & Payments)
Date: November 28, 2024
Audience: Support team, L2 agents

---

EDGE CASE 1: Customer loaded wallet but money not reflecting
Issue: Customer loads wallet via UPI. Money deducted from bank account but wallet balance not updated.
Root cause: Usually a 2–5 minute propagation delay. Occasionally, the wallet credit webhook fails.
Fix: If balance is not updated within 15 minutes, L1 can raise a wallet credit reconciliation ticket. System auto-credits within 2 hours in most cases. If not resolved in 4 hours, escalate to L2 for manual credit.
Refund alternative: If customer insists on reversal instead of waiting, the deducted amount is returned to the source bank account within T+1. Wallet credit is then cancelled. Customer must raise a fresh load after reversal.

EDGE CASE 2: Wallet-to-bank transfer failing for Full KYC customer
Issue: Customer has Full KYC wallet but wallet-to-bank transfer button is greyed out or failing.
Possible reasons:
a) Customer has hit the daily ₹10,000 or monthly ₹50,000 wallet-to-bank transfer limit.
b) Customer's linked bank account has been deregistered.
c) System glitch in the wallet-to-bank module (check incident board before diagnosing).
Fix: Check limits in CRM first. If limits are not exhausted, check if the linked bank account is still active. If all looks fine, raise a tech ticket.

EDGE CASE 3: Min KYC wallet holder trying to transfer to another person
Issue: Min KYC wallet holders frequently try to send money to friends or family via wallet. They get an error they don't understand.
Reason: Per RBI PPI guidelines, Min KYC wallets can only be used for merchant payments, not P2P transfers. This is not a FinBank restriction — it's an RBI mandate.
Fix: Explain the restriction clearly. Suggest the customer upgrade to Full KYC to unlock P2P transfers. Do not create a workaround or suggest using the merchant payment flow to transfer to a friend.

EDGE CASE 4: Wallet balance lost after account closure
Issue: Customer closed their account but wallet balance was not transferred before closure.
Fix: Wallet balance is held in escrow for 30 days after account closure. Customer can contact support within 30 days to receive a refund to their registered bank account. After 30 days, the balance transfer process requires L3 involvement and takes an additional 7 business days.

EDGE CASE 5: Cashback credited to wallet but customer wants it in bank account
Issue: FinBank cashback and rewards are credited to the wallet by default. Some customers want them in their savings account instead.
Current policy: Cashback and rewards are always credited to wallet. There is no option to route them to the savings account. This is a known customer pain point and is in the product roadmap for H2 2025.
Workaround: Customer can do a wallet-to-bank transfer (if Full KYC) to move the cashback balance to their bank account. Wallet-to-bank transfers are free.

EDGE CASE 6: Expired wallet balance
Issue: Min KYC wallet expires after 12 months of inactivity. Customer complains they lost the balance.
Fix: Wallet balance is NOT lost on expiry. It is frozen (wallet marked inactive). Customer must reactivate by completing Full KYC upgrade. After upgrade, balance is restored and is available in the Full KYC wallet. If the customer has already lost access to the registered mobile number, this requires L3 manual intervention.
