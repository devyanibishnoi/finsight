FinBank — INTERNAL POLICY
International Transaction Policy
Version: 2.3 | Last Updated: February 28, 2025 | Owner: Cards & Compliance Team

1. OVERVIEW
This policy governs the use of FinBank debit cards and UPI for international transactions, including enabling/disabling international usage, transaction limits, forex charges, and restrictions under FEMA.

2. DEFAULT SETTINGS
International usage on FinBank debit cards is disabled by default for all accounts.
This applies to: online purchases on foreign websites, POS transactions outside India, and ATM withdrawals abroad.
International UPI usage is not supported (UPI is currently India-domestic only per NPCI guidelines, except for select bilateral corridors with UAE, Singapore, and Bhutan as of 2024).

3. ENABLING INTERNATIONAL TRANSACTIONS
Eligibility: Tier 2 (Full KYC) accounts only. Tier 0 and Tier 1 accounts cannot enable international usage.
Process: In-app → Cards → International Transactions → Enable → Select valid duration.
Duration options: 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or Always On.
"Always On" requires a secondary verification step (Video KYC confirmation or Aadhaar OTP + Selfie).
Customers can disable international transactions at any time instantly via in-app.

4. INTERNATIONAL TRANSACTION LIMITS
ATM withdrawals abroad: ₹25,000 equivalent per day (subject to local ATM limits).
POS transactions abroad: ₹1,00,000 equivalent per day.
Online transactions on foreign websites: ₹1,00,000 equivalent per day.
Combined international limit: ₹1,00,000 per calendar day (all international channels combined).
LRS (Liberalised Remittance Scheme) annual limit: USD 2,50,000 per financial year per individual, as mandated by RBI.

5. FOREX CHARGES
Forex markup fee: 2.5% on the transaction amount (applied on top of the network exchange rate).
Cross-currency conversion is done at the Visa/Mastercard network rate at the time of settlement (typically T+2 from the transaction date). The rate at settlement may differ from the rate at the time of transaction.
ATM withdrawal fee abroad: ₹500 per transaction (flat fee, in addition to forex markup).
No fee for declining an international transaction.

6. BLOCKED COUNTRIES
Transactions to entities or individuals in FATF high-risk and non-cooperative jurisdictions are blocked. The current blocked list is maintained by the Compliance team and updated quarterly. Customers attempting transactions to blocked countries will see an error: "Transaction not permitted for this location."

7. FEMA COMPLIANCE
All international transactions are subject to the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA).
Prohibited uses under FEMA for Indian residents:
- Purchase of lottery tickets, banned magazines, football pools.
- Remittance for purchase of foreign securities not approved by SEBI.
- Payments to entities in countries identified as non-cooperative by FATF.
FinBank automatically blocks transactions falling into FEMA-prohibited categories at the merchant category code level.

8. TAX COLLECTED AT SOURCE (TCS) ON INTERNATIONAL SPENDS
As per Finance Act 2023, TCS of 20% is applicable on international card spends above ₹7,00,000 per financial year (including spending on international apps like Netflix, Amazon US).
FinBank reports TCS to the Income Tax Department as required. TCS is credited to the customer's Form 26AS and can be claimed as credit while filing ITR.
FinBank collects TCS at the time of the transaction on amounts exceeding the ₹7,00,000 annual threshold.

9. AGENT GUIDANCE
L1 agents can enable/disable international transactions on behalf of a customer (with identity verification) if the customer is unable to do so in-app. This is done via CRM → Cards → International Settings. L1 cannot override the Tier 2 eligibility requirement.
