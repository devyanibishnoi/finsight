FinBank — INTERNAL POLICY
Refund Policy
Version: 2.7 | Last Updated: December 20, 2024 | Owner: Customer Experience Team

1. OVERVIEW
This policy covers all scenarios in which a customer is entitled to a refund and defines the process, timeline, and mode of refund for each scenario. Refunds are categorized into system-initiated auto-refunds and agent-initiated manual refunds.

2. FAILED TRANSACTION REFUNDS (AUTO-REFUND)

2.1 UPI Failures
If a UPI transaction fails after money is debited, the system initiates an auto-reversal. Timeline:
- NPCI confirms failure within 1 hour: Refund credited within T+1 business day.
- NPCI status remains pending: Refund within T+5 business days.
- If not credited by T+5: Customer must raise a support ticket. Manual refund processed within 7 business days.
Refund mode: Credit to the same UPI-linked account from which the debit occurred.

2.2 IMPS / NEFT Failures
If beneficiary bank rejects the transfer: Funds auto-return within T+1 (IMPS) or T+2 (NEFT).
If transfer is stuck in pending: Customer raises ticket after 4 hours (IMPS) or 2 business days (NEFT). Trace request raised. Resolution within 5 business days.
Refund mode: Credit back to originating FinBank account.

2.3 Card Payment Failures
If merchant terminal times out and debit occurs: Auto-reversal typically within T+1 by payment network.
If not reversed by T+3: Customer raises ticket. Manual investigation and refund within 10 business days.

3. MERCHANT REFUNDS

3.1 Merchant-Initiated Refunds
When a merchant initiates a refund for returned goods or cancelled orders:
- Expected timeline per merchant: typically 5–7 business days.
- Timeline for credit to appear in FinBank account after merchant initiates: 2–3 business days.
- If refund not received within 10 business days of merchant confirmation: Customer raises ticket. FinBank follows up with payment network. Resolution within 7 additional business days.

3.2 Merchant Disputes (FinBank-Initiated Chargeback)
When FinBank raises a chargeback on customer's behalf (unauthorized transaction or non-delivery of goods):
- Chargeback process takes 30–60 calendar days.
- Provisional credit at FinBank's discretion for amounts above ₹5,000 pending chargeback resolution.
- If chargeback is decided against the customer, provisional credit is reversed. Customer is notified 3 days in advance.

4. SERVICE FEE REFUNDS
Incorrectly charged service fees: Refund processed within 2 business days of agent verification.
Annual fee refund for account closure within 30 days: Full refund. After 30 days: Pro-rated refund (not available after 6 months of charge date).

5. REFUND MODES
Refunds are always credited to the original payment source:
- Debited from FinBank account → Credited to same FinBank account.
- Debited from external bank via UPI → Credited back to same external bank account.
- FinBank does not issue refunds as cash, cheque, or to a different account than the source.

6. REFUNDS FinBank CANNOT PROCESS
- Transactions sent to a wrong beneficiary due to customer error (wrong UPI ID or account number).
- Transactions older than 180 days (6 months) from the transaction date.
- Refund requests for transactions marked as settled and undisputed for more than 90 days.
- Transactions where the customer voluntarily authorized the payment, unless fraud is proven.

7. AGENT GUIDANCE
L1 agents can process refunds up to ₹5,000 without additional approval. For refunds between ₹5,000 and ₹50,000, L2 approval is required. For refunds above ₹50,000, Finance team sign-off is mandatory.
