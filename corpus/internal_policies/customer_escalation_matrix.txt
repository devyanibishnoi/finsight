FinBank — INTERNAL POLICY
Customer Support Escalation Matrix
Version: 5.0 | Last Updated: March 15, 2025 | Owner: Customer Experience Team

1. OVERVIEW
This document defines the three-tier support structure, the scope of each tier, escalation criteria, and response SLAs. All support interactions must be logged in the CRM with complete notes. Escalation without a CRM log is not permitted.

2. TIER DEFINITIONS

L1 — Frontline Support
Staffing: Customer-facing agents, first point of contact.
Channels handled: In-app chat, email, social media DMs, helpline calls.
Scope: Account information queries, KYC status, transaction status, basic troubleshooting, guided flows for PIN reset, card block, UPI issues.
Authority:
- Process refunds up to ₹5,000.
- Initiate card blocks on customer request.
- Initiate debit freeze on verified customer request.
- Resend OTPs and account activation emails.
Cannot do: Override account blocks, waive charges, access backend risk data, initiate investigation tickets, modify loan terms.

L2 — Senior Support / Technical Resolution
Staffing: Senior agents with backend CRM access.
Escalation trigger: L1 unable to resolve within 24 hours, or issue falls outside L1 authority.
Scope: Dispute investigation, chargeback initiation, manual KYC review coordination, fraud case handling, account freeze/unfreeze (within policy), refunds up to ₹50,000.
Authority:
- Approve refunds ₹5,001 to ₹50,000.
- Waive bounce charges (once per loan lifetime).
- Initiate chargeback with payment networks.
- Schedule manual re-KYC for customers who failed automated verification twice.
- Coordinate with partner banks on stuck NEFT/IMPS transactions.
Response SLA from escalation: 4 business hours.

L3 — Specialist Teams
Sub-teams: Compliance, Fraud, Lending, Legal, Finance.
Escalation trigger: L2 unable to resolve within 48 hours, regulatory breach suspected, fraud confirmed, SLA breach, court orders, loan restructuring, unclaimed balance handling.
Scope: Regulatory reporting, law enforcement coordination, loan restructuring, nominee claim processing, account closure with balance disputes, fraud investigation.
Authority: Full system access and cross-team sign-off power.
Response SLA from escalation: 24 hours (initial response), resolution per case type.

3. ESCALATION PROCESS
L1 to L2: In CRM, select Escalate → L2 → Fill escalation reason (mandatory field). Customer notified automatically via SMS that their ticket has been escalated.
L2 to L3: Same process. L2 must add a case summary of all steps already taken. L3 will not accept a blind escalation.
Reverse escalation (L3 back to L2): L3 may return a case to L2 with specific instructions if it does not meet L3 criteria. L2 must action within 4 hours.

4. SLA SUMMARY
Issue Type | L1 SLA | L2 SLA | L3 SLA
Account query | 1 hour | N/A | N/A
Transaction status | 2 hours | 4 hours | N/A
Failed transaction refund | 4 hours | 24 hours | 5 business days
Fraud dispute | 4 hours (triage) | 48 hours | 10 business days
KYC failure | 2 hours | 24 hours | 3–5 business days
Loan issue | 2 hours | 24 hours | 5 business days
SLA breach compensation review | N/A | 4 hours | 24 hours

5. CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION STANDARDS
Every escalation must be followed by a proactive SMS to the customer with: ticket number, escalated team name, and updated expected resolution date.
If a resolution date is missed, L2 or L3 must communicate a new date within 2 hours of the breach. No customer should go more than 24 hours without an update on an open ticket.

6. COMPLAINTS TO RBI OMBUDSMAN
If a customer complaint is unresolved within 30 days, the customer has the right to escalate to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman. Agents must proactively inform customers of this right at the 25-day mark. FinBank's Nodal Officer details are available in-app and on the website.
