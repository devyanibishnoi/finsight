FinBank — INTERNAL POLICY
Account Freeze & Unfreeze Rules
Version: 2.4 | Last Updated: February 3, 2025 | Owner: Risk & Compliance Team

1. OVERVIEW
An account freeze restricts all or specific transactions on a customer account. This document defines freeze triggers, freeze types, and the process to restore account functionality. Agents must not manually freeze or unfreeze accounts without proper authorization level.

2. TYPES OF FREEZES
Full Freeze: All debits and credits blocked. Customer can view balance and statements but cannot transact.
Debit Freeze: Only outgoing transactions blocked. Incoming credits (salary, refunds) continue to work.
Credit Freeze: Only incoming transactions blocked. Customer can spend existing balance.

3. AUTOMATIC FREEZE TRIGGERS

3.1 Security Triggers
- 3 consecutive failed PIN or MPIN attempts within 30 minutes → Debit freeze for 4 hours, auto-releases.
- 5 consecutive failed PIN or MPIN attempts within 24 hours → Full freeze, requires manual unfreeze.
- Login detected from new device in different city within 2 hours of last login → Debit freeze for 2 hours (precautionary, auto-releases).
- OTP entered incorrectly 5 times in a single session → Full freeze for 24 hours.

3.2 KYC Triggers
- Tier 1 KYC expires after 12 months and customer has not re-verified → Debit freeze applied.
- KYC re-verification attempted and failed twice within 30 days → Full freeze, manual compliance review required.
- PAN-Aadhaar linking becomes invalid or unlinked post RBI deadline → Debit freeze until resolved.

3.3 Regulatory and Compliance Triggers
- Internal fraud model flags account (risk score above 85 out of 100) → Full freeze, compliance team reviews within 24 hours.
- Rapid successive transfers to 5 or more new beneficiaries within 1 hour → 12-hour debit freeze, auto-releases if no further flags.
- Court order or law enforcement directive received → Full freeze, requires legal team sign-off to unfreeze. No time limit.

4. MANUAL FREEZE (AGENT-INITIATED)
L2 and above agents can initiate a manual debit freeze on customer request, for example if the customer reports a lost or stolen phone. Process: CRM → Account Management → Initiate Freeze → Select Type → Log Reason. Duration: up to 30 days. Must be reviewed at the 15-day mark. L1 agents cannot initiate freezes; they must escalate to L2.

5. UNFREEZE PROCESS
Auto-releases (under 4 hours): Expire automatically, no agent action needed.
PIN failure (5 attempts): Customer completes identity re-verification via Aadhaar OTP on registered mobile, or Video KYC. Freeze lifts within 30 minutes of successful verification.
KYC expiry freeze: Customer completes re-KYC in-app. Freeze lifts within 30 minutes.
Fraud-flagged full freeze: Compliance team must clear the flag. SLA is 48 hours from freeze initiation.
Court order freeze: Cannot be unfrozen without written clearance from legal team.

6. CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION
All freeze events trigger an immediate SMS and in-app notification stating the freeze type and a general reason code. Specific fraud or investigation details are not disclosed in the notification. Customer can raise a support ticket directly from the freeze notification screen.

7. EDGE CASES
If account is frozen mid-transaction: the transaction rolls back and funds return to source within 2 business days. If a scheduled EMI debit hits a frozen account: EMI is marked as bounced and a ₹500 bounce charge is applied. Customer must raise a waiver request if the freeze was triggered by an error.
