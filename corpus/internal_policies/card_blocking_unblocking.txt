FinBank — INTERNAL POLICY
Card Blocking & Unblocking Policy
Version: 3.0 | Last Updated: February 14, 2025 | Owner: Cards & Payments Team

1. OVERVIEW
This policy governs when a debit or prepaid card is automatically or manually blocked, the process to unblock a card, and the card replacement procedure. All FinBank physical and virtual cards are subject to this policy.

2. AUTOMATIC CARD BLOCK TRIGGERS

2.1 PIN Failures
- 3 consecutive wrong PIN attempts at an ATM or POS terminal → Card blocked immediately.
- Unblock method: Customer must reset PIN via in-app before the card can be used again. ATM PIN reset using Aadhaar-linked OTP is also available.

2.2 Fraud Detection
- Transaction flagged by FinBank's fraud detection model as high-risk (score above 90/100) → Card temporarily blocked for 2 hours.
- If customer confirms the transaction in-app within the 2-hour window → Block lifted automatically.
- If customer does not respond within 2 hours → Block extended to 24 hours, customer notified.
- If customer marks the transaction as unauthorized → Card permanently blocked, replacement process initiated.

2.3 CVV / Expiry Failures
- 5 incorrect CVV entries on online transactions within 24 hours → Card blocked for online transactions for 24 hours.
- Card usage attempted after expiry date → Card blocked. Physical card replacement required.

2.4 Account Freeze
If the underlying account is placed under full freeze, all associated cards are automatically blocked for the duration of the freeze.

3. MANUAL CARD BLOCK (CUSTOMER-INITIATED)
Customers can block their card instantly via:
- In-app: Cards section → Block Card → Select Reason (Lost / Stolen / Suspicious Activity).
- Support chat: L1 agent can initiate block on behalf of the customer after identity verification.
- Helpline: After passing identity verification questions.
Blocked cards cannot be unblocked. A manual block triggers the card replacement process automatically.

4. TEMPORARY INTERNATIONAL BLOCK
International transactions are disabled by default. Customers can toggle international usage on/off in-app. If a transaction is attempted in a new country and international toggle is off → Transaction declined (card not blocked). Customer is notified and can enable international transactions in-app.

5. CARD UNBLOCKING PROCESS

5.1 PIN Failure Block
Customer resets PIN in-app using Aadhaar OTP or Video KYC confirmation. PIN reset takes effect immediately. Card usable within 10 minutes.

5.2 Fraud Detection Block (Temporary, 2-hour)
Customer confirms or denies the flagged transaction in-app. Confirmation lifts block immediately. Denial triggers permanent block + replacement.

5.3 Online CVV Block (24-hour)
Auto-releases after 24 hours. No action required by customer or agent.

6. CARD REPLACEMENT
Triggered by: Customer-initiated permanent block (lost/stolen), fraud-confirmed block, card damage, card expiry.
Replacement card delivery:
- Virtual card: Issued instantly via in-app. Usable immediately for online transactions.
- Physical card: Dispatched within 2 business days. Delivery in 5–7 business days (metro cities), 7–10 business days (non-metro).
- Physical card replacement fee: ₹200 (first replacement free per calendar year for Tier 2 accounts).
- Emergency physical card: Available in select cities via courier. Fee: ₹500. Delivery: 24–48 hours.
Old card is permanently deactivated at the moment replacement is initiated. All auto-pay mandates linked to the old card number must be updated manually by the customer.

7. AGENT GUIDANCE
L1 agents can: initiate manual card block, guide customers through PIN reset, check card status.
L1 agents cannot: override a fraud-triggered block, expedite physical card delivery, waive replacement fees (L2 approval required for fee waiver).
